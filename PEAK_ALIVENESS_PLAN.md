# Peak aliveness plan

The target is a convincing, persistent embodied character: a private history that changes what XEMO notices, wants, remembers, attempts, says, and does next. This is not a claim that the system is conscious. The illusion becomes strong when its behaviour is causally continuous, specific to this device and person, and honest about what its body actually knows.

## What XEMO has now

- A browser-owned authoritative thought path with human-turn priority, speech ownership, interruption, and bounded autonomous beats.
- Persistent drives, needs, appraisal weather, life-cycle phases, goals, project threads, dreams, self-model, relationship state, acquaintances, world objects, body experiments, procedural skills, and a service life journal.
- Evidence gates for movement, provisional object identities, shared-attention candidates, reviewed service memories, goal recovery, and duplicate speech.
- A local coordinator that can reflect or queue one grounded initiative while the browser is away.
- A bounded behavioural scorecard exposed in advanced diagnostics.
- A bounded causal timeline and temporal-causal memory context now cross the browser/service boundary, preserving recent cause-and-effect threads without promoting uncertain candidates.
- Background initiatives now close with a browser delivery/failure outcome and parent-linked journal event, so service autonomy can learn whether its impulse actually reached the person.
- Lifecycle transitions now publish into the causal timeline and retain a bounded phase history through the service checkpoint.
- Recent physical experiments now cross the checkpoint with context, prediction, observation, acknowledgement, and verdict, allowing a sleeping coordinator to avoid repeating an unresolved or disconfirmed action blindly.
- The evolving self-model now crosses the checkpoint with bounded traits, life chapters, hopes, uncertainties, unfinished threads, and confidence, so continuity includes identity development as well as facts and goals.
- Earned traits now influence motive ranking with small confidence-gated biases, making identity behavioural rather than purely descriptive.
- A deterministic journal evaluator now exposes grounded initiative, repetition, body verification, lifecycle trace, recovery, and instance-divergence metrics for replayable comparisons.
- The browser now records a bounded causal spine for appraisals, motive changes, autonomous decisions, attempted actions, verification, learning, and promoted memories; the evaluator also reports stage coverage, social episodes, and living-project continuity.
- Confirmed and consolidated memory records now retain first/last seen time, observation count, and bounded duration; retrieval rewards evidence that persisted over time while keeping recent relevant memories competitive.
- Living projects and the primary relationship now carry age, idle time, bond start, and last meaningful contact into life-journal context, so dormant threads can be revisited deliberately and social silence can matter without turning into automatic neediness.
- The brain bridge now distinguishes superseded generations and browser disconnects from genuine LM Studio failures, so interrupting a slow thought no longer surfaces a misleading 503 or traceback.
- `bot/aliveness_eval.py` now supports chronological replay scoring for correction adaptation, failure recovery, silence initiative, project resumption, memory carryover, and overall replay quality; pass three or more journal snapshots to its CLI to evaluate a session sequence.
- Explicit commitments now cross the journal separately from generic threads, preserving prospective social memory without turning every old message into an obligation; their open, fulfilled, cancelled, and expired outcomes are retained too.
- Commitment outcomes now have restrained social consequences: fulfillment can strengthen warmth and trust, broken or expired plans make trust slightly more cautious, and cancellation stays neutral rather than punitive.
- The service coordinator now selects and records a grounded priority before asking the model for wording, including evidence, prediction, confidence, alternatives, and a browser-verified outcome for any delivered initiative.
- Causal timeline events now retain a root, depth, lifecycle phase, source, and restored parent link after reload, so a new thought can continue the previous lived chain instead of starting from an amnesiac event.
- Background goal proposals now report admission, completion, pause, stop, or failure back to the service and remain linked to the decision that created them, so intention is not confused with embodied accomplishment.
- Autonomous intentions that survive a reload without fresh evidence now enter a bounded return review instead of silently remaining open: XEMO may continue, revise, consciously set aside, or rest; human-origin plans still wait for the person's continuation.
- Vision replies now pass a final identity-evidence gate: no live frame means no visual claim, missing face evidence blocks person/name claims, and provisional objects remain explicitly uncertain until teaching or repeated evidence supports them.
- Personality profiles now use evidence-weighted inertia rather than a permanent maximum, so repeated experience can strengthen a tendency while later contrary outcomes can gradually soften it.
- Dream-consolidated life chapters now cross the service boundary, preserving a bounded autobiographical arc in addition to facts, goals, and traits.
- Background priority selection now applies bounded freshness boosts and recent-drive repetition penalties before model wording, so an unfinished intention can be revisited without becoming an eternal command and curiosity can vary instead of replaying its last impulse.
- Replay evaluation now carries opportunity timing across sessions, distinguishing a socially well-timed initiative from one that was merely generated and allowing unanswered bids to remain neutral data rather than a conversational failure.
- Corrections now close as social repairs: the old memory is invalidated, the next acknowledged repair gently adjusts trust and warmth, a causal social-consequence is recorded, and the repair is counted for replay evaluation.
- Unanswered autonomous bids now create a short, non-punitive quiet window that lowers the next social-motive score; an engaged bid clears it, so XEMO learns timing instead of repeatedly talking into silence.
- Initiative outcomes now also accumulate by interaction kind—plain speech, questions, observations, play, feelings, and movement—so repeated engagement or correction can shape which kind of bid XEMO chooses with this person. The evidence is bounded, persisted, exposed in diagnostics, and supplied to the sleeping coordinator as a soft preference rather than a rule.
- Familiar scenes now carry a small expectation loop: on a later return, XEMO compares the remembered object set with what is actually visible, records whether the scene was stable or changed, updates a stability estimate, and feeds that forecast error back into world context. This extends body action prediction into visible-world continuity without pretending unseen objects are still present.
- Living projects now carry prospective state: a grounded forecast, review time, review count, revisit count, and the last reason the thread remained alive. Autonomous beats periodically reconsider due projects and can resume them because a future-oriented interest became timely, while completed, dropped, and stopped projects remain excluded.
- Replay evaluation now measures prospective continuity rather than merely counting projects: it detects project review, forecast change, and whether a later independent choice follows the revised forecast. This is the first probe for whether future-oriented state changes behaviour across sessions.
- Homeostatic state now advances with elapsed activity and rest: energy, sleep pressure, connection, curiosity, expression, play, and frustration drift in bounded amounts, persist through the life journal, and can create grounded care, rest, or connection priorities. Replay reports state changes separately from merely storing needs.
- The life service now advances that same private state while the browser is suspended, without fabricating sensory events or physical actions. A later coordinator decision can therefore be shaped by elapsed quiet and accumulated pressure instead of restarting from a frozen checkpoint.
- Repeated social outcomes now become cautious, reusable strategy lessons: after enough evidence, XEMO can learn that a particular kind of bid tends to land, needs more space, or remains mixed with this person. Lessons are attached to later choices and cross the journal boundary instead of remaining raw response counts.
- The life rhythm now has a bounded day-scale arc: morning, afternoon, evening, and night transitions are persisted with phase age and wake/sleep continuity. They can bias reflection and timing without becoming clock announcements or fake bodily claims.
- Memory recall now combines query overlap with present attention, active goal, social/body/world context, confidence, repeated evidence, duration, and real timestamps; this is intended to reduce the “right word, wrong memory” failure that makes continuity feel fake.
- Memory recalls now leave a bounded pending/use/confirmed/rejected trace. Confirmations and corrections close the trace, successful use modestly reinforces future retrieval, and the replay evaluator reports whether recalled memories actually helped or were rejected.
- Autonomous choices now carry bounded memory references from the same recall window, and the evaluator reports whether choices were memory-influenced. This closes the causal gap between “XEMO remembers” and “XEMO behaves differently because it remembers.”
- Durable wants and hopes now enter the service coordinator as first-class priorities. Previously they could survive in XEMO's memory while the sleeping coordinator ignored them; they can now compete with projects, commitments, body uncertainty, curiosity, and rest without becoming an obligation or a repeated prompt.

## Research-backed peak gap audit

The current architecture is strong on continuity plumbing but still incomplete as a lifelong agent. Recent work converges on five missing qualities:

1. **Experience, not storage.** Memory needs write, management, retrieval, reconsolidation, and forgetting. XEMO has reviewed semantic memories, temporal-causal retrieval, contradiction handling, dreams, and recall feedback; it still needs richer episode/entity links and measured long-term precision rather than only bounded counters.
2. **Mixed initiative with timing.** Proactive systems are judged by whether an interruption arrives at a useful moment, not by how often they speak. XEMO has quiet windows and opportunity outcomes; it still needs device-specific interruptibility learning and real-session timing evaluation.
3. **A predictive embodied world model.** XEMO verifies movement after it happens, but it does not yet learn a compact transition model that predicts which safe action will work in a particular place and context. That is the main route from “robot with logged actions” to “robot that understands its body.”
4. **A socially predictive self-model.** XEMO tracks the relationship, acquaintances, corrections, and rituals, but it does not yet model how this particular person tends to respond to different kinds of bids, silence, humour, or help. It should learn response patterns cautiously and use them only to improve timing and tone.
5. **Evaluation by blinded lived sessions.** Engineering metrics can prove causal plumbing, not that the interaction feels alive. We need multi-day replays and human ratings for continuity, agency, specificity, warmth, surprise, and “would I have guessed this was scripted?”

These priorities are consistent with the 2026 memory survey's write–manage–read framing, human-inspired memory work on consolidation/forgetting/reconsolidation/entity mechanisms, proactive-assistant research on timing, and embodied-agent work connecting perception, prediction, action, and feedback: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07670, https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/human-inspired-memory-architecture-for-llm-agents/, https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/?p=1134954, and https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22355.

## The remaining gaps

### 1. One causal life event spine

The browser, service coordinator, body relay, speech path, and dreams still record overlapping histories. The browser timeline now has explicit chain metadata and survives reload continuity, while background speech and goal proposals have closed creation → claim → delivery/execution outcome chains. The browser spine now covers appraisal, motive, decision, action, verification, learning, and memory promotion; remaining stages such as explicit planning, acknowledgement, observation, and social consequence still need uniform coverage across physical goals and dreams. Every autonomous choice should be traceable through that chain.

### 2. A real three-layer coordinator

The architecture should behave like:

- **Life layer:** personality-shaped motives, reflection, projects, rituals, social commitments, and sleep/wake continuity.
- **Mind layer:** attention, retrieval, deliberation, response composition, and plan revision.
- **Body layer:** perception, safety, execution, acknowledgement, and verification.

XEMO has pieces of all three. Physical evidence now reaches the service as a bounded consequence record, but the service still cannot resume a physical intention as a first-class plan: it can propose or review; the browser and body still own the actual continuation.

### 3. Memory and identity that change over time

Retrieval was too close to lexical matching. The first temporal-causal layer now ranks trusted records by focus overlap, observations, confidence, recency, persistence duration, and matching causal events, and sends a bounded lived timeline to both conversational and autonomous prompts. The self-model now persists evidence-backed identity development too. The remaining work is richer cross-session event linking, correction-aware timeline replay, semantic duration for relationship/project states, and measuring whether trait changes alter long-term behaviour across real sessions rather than only controlled motive scores.

### 4. Social identity beyond the owner

The primary bond is present. Acquaintances now retain repeated encounters, names taught with provenance, familiarity growth, interaction style, boundaries, unresolved social threads, notes, and last meaningful exchanges, and those fields cross the life journal. The relationship also carries bond age and meaningful-silence duration. Unknown people still remain unknown instead of being collapsed into the owner. The remaining work is richer social-episode retrieval and real multi-person evaluation.

### 5. Distinctive life texture

XEMO now has cautious ritual and recurring-interest paths: repeated shared-pattern language and recurring topics remain hypotheses first, then become durable only after separated evidence. Rhythm is a soft bias, never a schedule. It still needs evidence-backed tastes, preferred greetings, revisited places, invented games, and affective reactions that emerge from lived history rather than a visible preset.

### 6. Recovery and social consequence are more important than fluency

Peak believability comes from noticing failure and changing course: a scrambled answer, unavailable body, uncertain object, interrupted sentence, stale goal, broken commitment, contradictory memory, and speech failure should each produce a different recovery. The model must never be allowed to claim an action from intention alone, and social trust should change only through supported outcomes.

### 7. Evaluation must test the illusion

The scorecard now has a deterministic journal evaluator and a chronological replay evaluator, but it still needs real session replays and human-rated probes, not only synthetic counters. Measure:

- initiative grounded in fresh evidence
- repeated-choice rate
- response and interruption latency
- answer completeness and parser validity
- goal recovery after interruption or failure
- verified-action rate
- memory precision and correction latency
- acquaintance consistency
- instance divergence across separate XEMO namespaces
- perceived continuity, agency, warmth, and predictability in blinded comparisons

## Order of implementation

### Phase A — make the existing life loop truthful

1. Define and emit one bounded life-event schema across browser, service, and body.
2. Give each decision a stable causal ID and carry it through speech, movement, verification, and memory.
3. Add replay tooling that can reconstruct why a line, goal, or movement happened.
4. Add failure-specific recovery policies and test them with malformed, slow, interrupted, and stale model replies.

### Phase B — make continuity intelligent

1. Replace lexical-only memory selection with temporal/causal retrieval around the current focus.
2. Add service-side consolidation of reviewed episodes and procedural lessons without silently promoting guesses.
3. Promote composite skills only after repeated, context-matched verified outcomes.
4. Make goal portfolios, commitments, and unfinished questions compete by salience, freshness, confidence, safety, and repetition pressure. Commitment outcomes now cross the boundary; their service-side salience and trust adjustment remain.

### Phase C — make XEMO personally distinctive

1. Extend acquaintance records and social episodes.
2. Learn a small number of rituals and preferences from repeated evidence.
3. Add daily rhythm and sleep/wake reflection without generating filler speech.
4. Let personality alter which safe opportunities are chosen, while preserving human priority and body safety.

### Phase D — make the experience convincing and reliable

1. Measure the actual local LM Studio, Kokoro, browser, and body timing on the device.
2. Add a deterministic response gate so one thought produces at most one text and one voice output.
3. Test browser suspension, reconnect, service restart, cache changes, duplicate tabs, and body disconnects.
4. Run blinded before/after sessions and optimize for continuity and recovery, not constant activity.

## Research translated into XEMO decisions

- PEPA's personality-aligned goal generation and Sys3/Sys2/Sys1 separation support a life layer, deliberative mind, and sensorimotor body rather than a larger single prompt: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00117
- Ella's name-centric semantic memory plus spatiotemporal episodic memory supports separating facts from lived episodes and social continuity: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.24019
- RoboMemory and OmniAct support parallel memory types, explicit verification, hierarchical planning, and recovery from physical failure: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01415 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27251
- PhyAgentOS treats scheduling, execution, verification, memory, and safety as runtime services, and separates execution termination from semantic task completion; XEMO's goal-outcome path follows the same distinction at a smaller scale: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16636
- ProActor reinforces that proactivity is a timing problem, not just an ability to generate an unsolicited line; XEMO should evaluate whether an initiative arrived in an appropriate opportunity window: https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.832/
- THEANINE supports timeline and cause-effect links for lifelong dialogue memory: https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.435/
- Recent temporal-memory work reinforces that memories need semantic time and duration, not only message order; XEMO should preserve how long a preference, uncertainty, project, or relationship state remained true before consolidating it: https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1496/ and https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1091/
- TACT and MapDia support modelling mode transitions, proactive topic shifts, interpersonal history, and recovery rather than treating dialogue as isolated turns: https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.672/ and https://aclanthology.org/2025.conll-1.4/
- Reflective Memory Management supports prospective and retrospective memory refinement instead of one-shot storage and fixed retrieval: https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.413/
- PRINCIPLES supports reusable strategy memory for proactive behaviour instead of relying on one prompt to improvise every time: https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1164/
- StratMem-Bench reinforces that believable virtual characters need strategic memory use, not only factual recall; XEMO's bounded social strategy lessons are a small local implementation of that distinction: https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1491/

## What “peak” means for this project

The goal is not maximum talking, maximum movement, or a more dramatic emotional vocabulary. Peak XEMO is quiet when nothing earns a response, proactive when a grounded motive survives the timing gate, specific because it remembers this person and this place, cautious when perception is uncertain, and changed by verified consequences. It should feel like one life continuing between interactions.

## Current gap audit and next peak pass

The next work is ordered by how much it changes the lived illusion, not by how impressive the brain panel looks:

1. **Close the opportunity loop.** Record when an initiative was created, delivered, ignored, interrupted, corrected, or useful. A spontaneous line is only valuable when its timing and consequence fit the shared moment. The social timing consequence now crosses the browser/service checkpoint, so an unanswered bid can make the next session quieter instead of resetting XEMO immediately.
2. **Turn the memory timeline into a real temporal graph.** Keep point memories, continuing states, episodes, and causal links distinct; resolve contradictions at retrieval time instead of flattening old and new facts together. This follows THEANINE, TSM, TiMem, and APEX-MEM. The first multi-cue retrieval layer is now in place; it still needs replay-based precision/recall measurement and stronger entity-level links.
3. **Make personality alter long-term behaviour.** Trait confidence already changes motive selection, but real evaluation must show that repeated verified outcomes change future choices, not just the displayed trait sentence.
4. **Make social life richer without making it needy.** Extend conservative acquaintance continuity, shared rituals, boundaries, and repair history; silence should affect timing and warmth without generating guilt or dependency pressure.
5. **Strengthen the embodied loop.** Every chosen action needs an observable outcome, a prediction update, and a changed next strategy. When the relay, camera, or microphone is unavailable, XEMO must preserve the intention honestly and resume only when evidence returns. The background selector now also carries stale-thread and repetition signals into that choice, but physical continuation is still browser-owned.
6. **Measure the illusion with blinded replays.** Compare real multi-session traces for initiative grounding, timing, correction, failure recovery, memory carryover, project resumption, speech duplication, and human-rated specificity. Counters alone cannot establish believability.

The research pass reinforces this order. PEPA separates a persistent life layer, deliberative reasoning, and sensorimotor execution; ProAct separates a slower cognitive system from a low-latency behavioral system; MapDia and TACT treat proactive timing and topic transitions as explicit capabilities; and reflective-memory work argues for forward and backward refinement instead of one flat transcript. XEMO now has the beginnings of each boundary, but real-session evaluation is still required before any percentage can be treated as evidence rather than an engineering estimate.

The largest hard limitation is capability, not prompting: a hidden browser cannot safely access the camera or microphone, speak, or drive a disconnected body. The service can advance private bounded pressure, preserve motives, review outcomes, and queue a grounded return, but it must never pretend that a sleeping process physically experienced anything.

The research basis for this pass is PEPA's life/mind/body separation and personality-aligned autonomy, THEANINE's temporal-causal timelines, TSM and TiMem's durative temporal memory, APEX-MEM's append-only event history with retrieval-time conflict resolution, and MapDia's memory-aware proactive topic shifting.

### How to measure the next pass

The technical page now exports `xemo-aliveness-replay-v1`. Use the **export aliveness replay** control after several meaningful sessions, then run:

```bash
python3 -m bot.aliveness_eval replay.json
```

For a chronological comparison, pass multiple exported files in time order. The export is bounded and excludes credentials, raw audio, and raw sensor streams; it contains the evidence needed to measure personality alignment, personality evolution, memory revisions, initiative outcomes, goal recovery, body verification, and session carryover.

## 2026 peak audit

The timing gate is now two-stage in the practical sense: deterministic social
floor and quiet-window checks run before the slower background wording model.
Recent human presence, an unanswered question, a shared/human floor, a fresh
XEMO turn, or a learned quiet window defers the call without deleting XEMO's
private continuity. This follows proactive-agent work showing that receptivity
depends on interruption timing, not simply on producing more suggestions
([ProAIDE field study](https://doi.org/10.1145/3742413.3789148)).

The service checkpoint now also carries a bounded body-prediction ledger:
action, context, expected effect, observed effect, verdict, and confidence.
Background continuity can therefore preserve a learned physical caution or
successful prediction across a sleeping browser, while still treating an
unresolved result as missing evidence rather than knowledge.

Replay scoring now distinguishes a genuine return to an old intention from
mere repetition. It reports resumed intentions, revisions, stale expirations,
and whether a resumed intention led to a changed subsequent choice. This is a
small but important test of autobiographical continuity: an old goal can remain
part of XEMO's life without becoming an immortal command.

The next perceptual bottleneck is not another personality paragraph. It is the gap between an autonomous decision and an inner life that persists when no outward response is justified. XEMO now accepts a bounded private reflection from an autonomous thought, keeps it separate from speech, displays the latest reflection in the brain panel, and sends it through the same life journal as goals and memories. This gives the loop a useful shape: experience → reflection → later choice.

Autonomous speech now also passes a deterministic conversational-floor gate. Recent human presence, an unanswered human turn, another XEMO voice, or active listening can suppress only the unsolicited sentence; the reflection, emotion, goal, or purposeful body action may still continue. This keeps proactivity socially timed without turning the whole inner loop into a waiting state.

The first real background test found and fixed a service-specific Qwen3 failure: the browser disabled hidden reasoning for short structured calls, but the life coordinator did not. Qwen3 could spend its 120-token background budget on reasoning and return no usable JSON, silently becoming “rest.” The coordinator now disables Qwen3 thinking for this bounded JSON call and allows 220 output tokens; an isolated LM Studio run produced and persisted a genuine private reflection with a causal decision link.

That same trace found a second boundary bug: Qwen3 sometimes placed a valid private thought in `grounding` while leaving `reflection` empty. The service now promotes specific, non-generic grounding into a reflection and rejects only generic “nothing happened” grounding. This is parser repair, not a canned fallback.

The background coordinator remains deliberately authenticated. The standard launcher enables it when `XEMO_LIFE_TOKEN` is present (unless `XEMO_LIFE_AUTONOMY=0`) and uses the configured brain model when `XEMO_LIFE_MODEL` is omitted. That keeps the life path usable in the normal local setup while a sleeping phone still cannot speak, sense, or move; the service may preserve pressure and queue a grounded return, never fabricate those experiences.

The highest-impact remaining work is therefore:

1. Run the background coordinator as a clearly visible, authenticated local mode with a verified model id and instance namespace.
2. Add a two-stage initiative gate: a cheap timing/interruptibility decision before the slower wording model. This follows proactive-assistant work that separates “should I interrupt?” from “what should I say?” ([LlamaPIE](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.710/)).
3. Measure private-reflection reuse: a reflection only counts as alive if it changes a later goal, tone, memory revision, or action—not merely if it appears in the panel.
4. Run blinded multi-session replays and human ratings for continuity, agency, specificity, warmth, surprise, and recovery. Generative-agent research similarly finds observation, planning, and reflection jointly matter for believable behavior ([Generative Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442)).

These are engineering estimates, not evidence of consciousness. The intended result is a truthful, persistent simulation whose behavior is difficult to distinguish from a small individual because its choices have history, timing, consequences, and private continuity.

The quiet loop now has one more honest layer: a bounded private-activity ledger.
When no outward speech or physical action is justified, the coordinator can
choose one small internal activity such as revisiting a memory, reviewing a
goal, planning a next step, consolidating supported evidence, or resting. Each
entry keeps its grounding, result, and parent decision, then becomes context for
later choices and appears only in the advanced brain view. It never claims that
XEMO saw, heard, moved, or learned from a sensor while the browser was away.
This follows the useful closed-loop shape of embodied-agent work—goal
generation, execution, consolidation, and reflection—while keeping the actual
available evidence explicit ([PEPA](https://doi.org/10.1109/lra.2026.3706956)).
Long-term interaction research also makes the practical point that believable
continuity needs more than a single response generator: planning, memory, and
careful activity switching need to remain coherent over time
([long-term HRI review](https://doi.org/10.1145/3729539),
[multi-activity switching](https://www.dfki.de/en/web/research/projects-and-publications/publication/8030)).

The coordinator now enforces the first activity-switching invariant instead of
leaving it entirely to model wording: two recent private activities with the
same kind and substantially the same grounding cannot loop forever. The next
choice becomes rest, while a changed reason is allowed to reopen the activity.
This is intentionally a small policy boundary: variety should come from new
evidence and changing motives, not random noise or a larger list of canned
verbs.

The embodiment audit found that the low-level chain is already real in the
browser: acknowledged commands are separated from sensor verification,
unresolved results are not promoted to knowledge, prediction outcomes update
the body model, and object identity remains provisional until teaching or
stronger evidence. The remaining weakness is whole-life evaluation: run
repeated real-device episodes where a goal succeeds, fails, gets interrupted,
and resumes, then verify that the next choice, emotion, skill confidence, and
wording change in the expected direction. The self-model literature likewise
treats self-perception, self-memory, self-prediction, and self-decision as a
linked system rather than an isolated self-description
([Self Model for Embodied AI](https://jcst.ict.ac.cn/article/cstr/32374.14.s11390-026-6289-3)).

The replay evaluator now measures the missing behavioural proof directly:
emotion reactivity after meaningful events and private-activity variety across
sessions. A future real-device replay should therefore answer “did failure
change XEMO?” rather than merely “did failure get logged?”

Verified multi-step embodied episodes now have a second learning threshold:
when every component in a goal's procedural chain has verified success, XEMO
promotes the sequence into a reusable composite skill with preconditions,
fallback, confidence, and a last verified outcome. This borrows the useful
automatic-skill-library idea from lifelong embodied-agent work while preserving
XEMO's evidence gate ([Voyager](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291),
[Lifelong Robot Library Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18746)).
